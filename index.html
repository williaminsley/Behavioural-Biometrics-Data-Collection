Behavioural Biometrics Study

This study is designed to collect pseudonymised behavioural interaction data for academic research into continuous authentication.

What you will do

You will complete two short tasks:

1. Typing task: type prompted words for 60 seconds.

2. Tapping task: tap moving targets for 60 seconds.

What to expect

The full session takes 2 minutes.

Before the tasks, you will be asked a few quick context questions such as time of day, fatigue, and input device.

At the end, you will see your results and your Participant ID.

Important instructions

Please complete the tasks naturally, using your normal typing and tapping behaviour.

For the typing task, type the word shown and press the Enter/Return key after each word.

Please do not take part while driving or in any unsafe situation.

Consent

This study collects pseudonymised behavioural, contextual, and device interaction data from short typing and tapping tasks for academic research into behavioural biometrics and continuous authentication.

What will be collected

We collect data generated while you use this study, including:

Typing data: key press and key release timings, dwell time, inter-key timings, typing errors, backspaces, prompted word timing, bigram timing features, and the text you type in response to the prompts.

Tapping and pointer/touch data: tap timing, hit/miss outcomes, target interaction timing, pointer movement, pointer down/up events, movement path features, and touch-contact information where available on your device/browser (for example touch radius, force, width, height, area, or rotation angle).

Context data: your selected time of day, fatigue rating, input device, vibration/movement setting, and alcohol response.

Device/browser data: browser user agent, platform, screen size, pixel ratio, timezone offset, Participant ID, session ID, and session timing metadata.

Important note about typed text

The words you type during the typing task may be stored as part of the research dataset.

Please do not type personal information, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other sensitive information. Only type the prompted study words.

What is not intended to be collected

We do not ask for your name or email address as part of the study task.

We do not intend to collect direct personal identifiers through the study interface. However, because behavioural data, device metadata, and raw typed responses may be stored, this study should be understood as collecting pseudonymised rather than fully anonymous data.

How your data is used

Your data will be used for academic research on behavioural biometrics, continuous authentication, and related machine learning analysis.

Uploaded session files may be processed into derived research datasets and statistical features for analysis, modelling, and dissertation reporting.

This project is conducted as part of a University of Nottingham research project, with supervisory input from Zally AI.

Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page before finishing.

If you wish to withdraw your uploaded data, contact: ppywi1@nottingham.ac.uk and include the Participant ID shown on the Results screen.

18+ only.

Do not take part while driving or in any unsafe situation.

Browser requirement: please complete this study in a normal Safari or Chrome tab.

Do not use private/incognito mode. Private browsing can interfere with persistent browser storage, which is needed to keep the same Participant ID on this device across sessions.
